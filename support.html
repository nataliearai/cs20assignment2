Support Us

Not ready to adopt? You can still make a real difference for the animals in our care.

Ways to help

  • Volunteer. Walk dogs, socialize cats, clean kennels, or help at adoption events. Volunteers must be 16 or older.
  • Foster. Give an animal a temporary home while they recover from surgery or wait for a spot at the shelter. We provide food and supplies.
  • Donate supplies. We always need towels, blankets, canned cat food, and unopened bags of dog food.
  • Give financially. Every dollar goes directly toward food, medical care, and shelter for our animals.
  • Spread the word. Share our adoptable animals with friends and family. The right home might be one share away.

Interested in volunteering or fostering? Send us a message and we will get you started.

What your donation provides

Donation What it covers
$10 A week of food for one cat
$25 Vaccinations for one new arrival
$50 A microchip and first vet exam
$100 Spay or neuter surgery for one animal
$250 One month of care for a foster dog
